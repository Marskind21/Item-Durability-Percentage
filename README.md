# Item Durability Percentage

This is an utility resourcepack to show the percentage of durability left on items.

![Five netherite pickaxes with durability percentages of 1%, 25%, 50%, 75% and 99%](images/pickaxes.png)

This pack also includes a stack indicator for bundles (counting from 1 to 64) and different textures for crossbows depending on what arrow is currently loaded inside it.

![an uncolored bundle with 11 items, a purple bundle with 27 items, a green bundle with 45 items and a red bundle with 64 items inside](images/bundles.png)

![a crossbow with an arrow loaded, a crossbow with a firework rocked loaded, a crossbow with a spectral arrow loaded and three crossbows with a red, green and blue tipped arrow loaded](images/crossbows.png)

*Note: This is a small part of my personal resourcepack that I've extracted and made available to the public.*
